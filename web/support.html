Support

Point Last Seen is a field app for search team training drills.

Every drill uses made-up information. Nobody is missing.

Email stevefunds2006@gmail.com about a problem with the app, an abusive post or member, or deleting your data.

Help, questions or a data request: open an issue at https://github.com/StephenSook/point-last-seen/issues. Don't post personal details in an issue.

When the command post website hits an error, it sends an error report with none of your text in it; the privacy policy says what a report holds.

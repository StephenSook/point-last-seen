## Already changed by the machine reviews

Both reviews agreed, or the code's own rules settled it:

- Dropped from the Spanish word list as everyday English: con, ya, mi (miles), arroyo, sin, hay, do, se (southeast), as.
- Numbers: a hundreds word takes the tens and units after it (doscientos treinta y cuatro is one group, 234); un and una count only after y (treinta y un); the split spellings veinte y dos and diez y seis count.
- Directions: este alone also means "this", so east counts only as al este, hacia el este, rumbo al este, del este or oriente.
- Added: craneos, tumbas; colorados, coloradas, anaranjados, anaranjadas, lila, lilas; localice, localizo, localizaron, ubicamos; pantalon, pantalones, playera, camiseta, audifonos.
- Removed: cafecito (it means a small coffee).
- Radio: read a number back digit by digit and a direction as a whole word (sesenta and setenta, este and oeste, noreste and noroeste are easy to mishear).
- Sentences: "Su hijo está en el cuatro cero cuatro ..." became "Llame a su hijo al ..."; added "Iba hacia el oeste por el sendero" and the number-first address.
